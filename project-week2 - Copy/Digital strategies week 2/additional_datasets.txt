European Social Survey (ESS) subset
===================================
Individual-level survey responses: age (continuous), gender (binary), education level (ordinal), left-right political scale, trust in government etc.
Lots of interesting data! The 'essurvey' R package gives immediate access!

install.packages("essurvey")

Country-level indicators (World Bank data)
==========================================
more than 150 countries, several country-level data: WDI. Also: gapminder can supplement this nicely!
And combining WDI and Gapminder data in common dataframe is a nice exercise (you will need joins, either
in sql or with left_join() in rtiny.

install.packages("WDI")       # World Bank indicators
install.packages("gapminder") # clean subset, ready to use

General social survey data (US)
===============================
Individual-level survey data from the US: demographics, political attitudes etc etc. Very Gower-suitable.
Available via gssr package:

install.packages("gssr")
